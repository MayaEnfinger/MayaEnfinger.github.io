In the Shadow of Bigfoot

This artwork was made for the song In the Shadow of Bigfoot by Shadowyze. I made this in Procreate and went for a creepy silhouette of Bigfoot in the woods. I also wanted to convey a more mysterious illustration for this song. I felt that the use of deep purples worked best to achieve this. This artwork was featured in FATE Magazine.
