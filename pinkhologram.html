Maya Enfinger, Pink Hologram , 2026, Digital Animation

Pink Hologram

This animation was made to exhibit the principle of animation: solid drawing. It was a challenge because for this technique to produce a smooth 360 rotation, all the facial features need to be aligned. I did the technique where you draw the head from different angles and draw lines to make sure everything looks right. I animated the hair seperately and without guides because I felt that this made it more natural.
